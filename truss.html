Commercial floor plan inventory, undisclosed lender (2024)

Truss

Banks lend trillions against physical assets they cannot see. Every asset-backed commercial loan in North America is secured by inventory — equipment fleets, vehicle floors, industrial machinery — and OCC regulations mandate on-site verification at least quarterly. That requirement has not changed in forty years. The method has not either: a human examiner, a clipboard, and a camera roll.

The labor pool is retiring. Fraud losses are nine figures and climbing. We propose replacing the clipboard with a guided mobile capture app, a VLM inference pipeline that reads serial plates and scores condition against reference libraries, and a bank-formatted audit report delivered in two days, not two weeks. The audit trail becomes machine-verifiable. The examiner's time per asset drops by 85%. The switching cost becomes the data.
